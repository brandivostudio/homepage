The team behind the growth numbers

Brandivo Studio is a performance marketing agency built by former in-house marketers who were tired of agencies that optimized for retainers instead of results.

Our story

Started by marketers who wanted better reporting

Brandivo Studio was founded in Bengaluru by a small team of in-house growth marketers who kept running into the same problem: agencies that were great at spending budget and vague about what it actually produced.

We built the agency we wished we'd had — one specialist per channel, reporting that maps straight to revenue, and strategy that changes when the data says it should. Today we work with SaaS companies, D2C brands and local businesses across India and beyond.

Our Mission

To make world-class digital marketing accessible to growing businesses through transparent strategy, honest reporting and work that's judged on revenue — not vanity metrics.

Our Vision

A future where every client can see, in plain language, exactly how their marketing budget turns into pipeline — and trusts the team managing it.
