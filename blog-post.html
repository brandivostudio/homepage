10 Technical SEO Fixes That Move Rankings Fastest

By Kavya Reddy · Sep 12, 2026 · 7 min read

Most sites don't need a content overhaul to move rankings — they need a handful of technical fixes cleared out of the way first. In this guide, we cover the ten fixes our SEO team reaches for first on almost every new client audit, in the order they're worth tackling.

1. Fix crawl budget waste

Large sites often let search engines crawl thousands of low-value URLs — filtered category pages, internal search results, staging pages — at the expense of pages that actually matter. Cleaning up your robots.txt and canonical tags redirects that crawl budget where it counts.

2. Resolve Core Web Vitals issues

Page speed is both a ranking factor and a conversion factor. Largest Contentful Paint and Cumulative Layout Shift issues are usually caused by unoptimized images and render-blocking scripts — both fixable without a full rebuild.

3. Consolidate duplicate content

Faceted navigation, tracking parameters and printer-friendly pages quietly create thousands of near-duplicate URLs. Canonical tags and parameter handling in Search Console clean this up quickly.

4. Fix broken internal links

Every broken internal link is a dead end for both users and crawlers. A quarterly internal link audit catches these before they accumulate.

Technical SEO doesn't get credit the way a new blog post does, but it's usually the highest-leverage work on the list.

5. Add missing structured data

Schema markup won't move rankings directly, but it unlocks rich results that dramatically improve click-through rate for the rankings you already have.

6. Rebuild your XML sitemap logic

Sitemaps should only include indexable, canonical URLs. A bloated sitemap full of redirects and noindex pages actively confuses crawlers.

7. Audit mobile usability

With mobile-first indexing, what search engines see is your mobile experience. Tap targets, viewport configuration and mobile page speed all deserve their own audit pass.

8. Clean up redirect chains

Multiple hops between a redirected URL and its final destination waste crawl budget and dilute link equity. Redirects should point straight to the final URL.

9. Strengthen internal linking to priority pages

Your highest-value commercial pages should never be more than a few clicks from the homepage. Internal linking is the cheapest authority-building lever most sites underuse.

10. Monitor indexation, not just rankings

None of the above matters if your important pages aren't indexed. A monthly indexation check in Search Console should be part of every SEO program.

None of these fixes require new content. Combined, they're usually responsible for the fastest ranking movement we see in the first 90 days of an engagement — which is exactly why they come before content strategy, not after.
