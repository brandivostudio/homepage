How Verve Skincare turned stalled SEO into its top revenue channel

A technical SEO overhaul and an intent-mapped content engine took organic search from an afterthought to the brand's single largest acquisition channel.

ClientVerve Skincare
Project Duration8 months
ServicesSEO, Content Strategy
IndustryBeauty & Personal Care

The challenge

Verve Skincare had been publishing blog content consistently for over a year, but organic traffic had plateaued and almost none of it converted into email signups or sales. A technical audit revealed the real issue: slow page speed, thin category pages, and content written for topics with little to no actual search demand.

The strategy

We rebuilt the SEO foundation before touching content volume. Site architecture was restructured around core product categories, Core Web Vitals issues were resolved, and a keyword map was built around actual buyer search intent rather than broad awareness topics.

  • Technical audit and fix of crawl, indexing and speed issues
  • Category and product page restructuring around search intent
  • An editorial calendar targeting commercial and informational keywords
  • A digital PR program to rebuild domain authority

Implementation

Technical fixes shipped in the first six weeks, followed by a rolling content calendar of two articles and one category page update per week. Every piece of content was mapped to a specific keyword cluster and funnel stage, with internal linking used to pass authority to commercial pages.

"We'd tried content marketing before, but nobody had connected it to an actual SEO strategy. Within two quarters, organic became our biggest channel — and it's stayed there."

Results

Within eight months, organic traffic had more than tripled, and — critically — that traffic was converting. Qualified leads from organic search grew even faster than traffic itself, a sign the new content was reaching the right audience.
